Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Customer Hunt respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use our platform, how that information is used, how it may be shared, and the choices available to you.

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account or use Customer Hunt, information may be collected to provide and operate the platform.

This information may include:

  • Account information: Your name, email address, and account credentials.
  • Product information: The products or services you enter and the target countries you select.
  • Platform activity: Information about your product searches, saved leads, account usage, and interactions with the platform.
  • Technical information: Information such as browser type, device information, IP address, and diagnostic data where collected.
  • Payment information: Subscription and payment-status information associated with your account.

2. How We Use Information

Customer Hunt uses information for purposes connected with operating and improving the platform.

  • Creating and managing user accounts.
  • Providing access to account features.
  • Processing product hunting requests.
  • Organising and displaying discovered leads.
  • Managing subscription access and billing status.
  • Maintaining platform security.
  • Detecting errors, abuse, and unauthorised activity.
  • Responding to customer support requests.

3. Public Lead Information

Customer Hunt is designed to discover publicly available online discussions and buying-intent signals that may be relevant to products and services entered by users.

Information displayed as a lead may include the original public source link, relevant text, and information necessary to identify the discovery.

Leads link back to their original sources where available. Users should review the original source and respect the rules and privacy expectations of the website where the information was published.

Customer Hunt is not the publisher or owner of third-party posts and websites linked through discovered leads.

4. Payments and Subscriptions

Customer Hunt offers subscription features that may require payment.

Payments are processed through Getly or another payment provider designated by Customer Hunt. Payment processing may require the provider to collect and process payment information.

Customer Hunt may receive information such as payment confirmation, subscription status, transaction references, and renewal or cancellation status.

Payment information is also subject to the privacy policy of the payment provider.

5. Information Sharing

Information may be shared when necessary to provide and maintain Customer Hunt.

  • Service providers: Providers that support hosting, databases, payment processing, security, and platform operations.
  • Legal requirements: Where disclosure is required by applicable law or a valid legal process.
  • Security: Where sharing is reasonably necessary to investigate fraud, abuse, or threats to the platform.
  • Business changes: In connection with a merger, acquisition, restructuring, or transfer of business assets, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Information is not made publicly visible merely because a user creates an account. Public lead information remains linked to its original third-party source.

6. Data Security

Customer Hunt uses technical and organisational measures intended to protect information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or loss.

Access to account information and platform systems should be restricted to authorised purposes.

Users are responsible for keeping their account passwords confidential and notifying Customer Hunt if they suspect unauthorised access to their accounts.

7. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as necessary to provide the platform, maintain account records, administer subscriptions, resolve disputes, maintain security, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Retention periods may differ depending on the type of information and the reason it is held.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights concerning your personal information.

  • Request access to personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion where legally applicable.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Request a copy of certain information.
  • Withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.

To make a privacy request, contact Customer Hunt using the contact details in the final section of this policy. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

9. Children's Privacy

Customer Hunt is intended for people who are at least 18 years old. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children under 18.

If you believe a child has provided personal information through the platform, contact us so that the matter can be reviewed.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Customer Hunt develops, introduces features, or responds to legal and operational requirements.

The updated policy will be published on this page with a revised update date.

11. Contact Customer Hunt

For privacy questions, personal information requests, or concerns about this policy, contact Customer Hunt using the email address below.
